# VeriPeers Launchpad

Build the official VeriPeers Coming Soon website according to the attached master specification document (VERIPEERS — OFFICIAL COMING SOON WEBSITE) and the attached official logo (VP WA LOGO (1).png).

Key requirements:
1. Brand Identity & Visual Direction:
- White-first editorial theme (75–85% pure white/light neutral canvas, not predominantly green, no dark-mode defaults).
- Brand color accents: Emerald Green (#006B4F), Deep Forest Green (#064E3B), Navy Blue (#073B63), Premium Gold (#C69A32), Light Gold (#E4C46B), Main Text (#18251F), Secondary Text (#66736C), Light Borders (#E3E9E5).
- Use the attached transparent official logo in the header and footer with proper proportions and clear space.
- Subtle, refined micro-interactions and smooth section scrolling.

2. Page Structure & Content:
- Header: Logo, navigation (Our Vision, For Founders, For Mentors, AI & Technology Resources, Partnerships), and "Get Early Access" CTA.
- Hero Section: Two-column editorial layout. Eyebrow "THE NEXT CHAPTER OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP", headline "People Move Potential Forward.", subheadline "Where Founders, Mentors, Mentees & Opportunities Connect.", launch announcement with countdown timer targeting October 20, 2026, dual CTAs ("Join the Early Interest List", "Explore Partnerships"), and a bespoke network/connection visual composition.
- Ecosystem Pillars Section: "More Than Connections. A World of Possibilities." featuring four distinct pillars: Founders, Mentors & Industry Experts, Mentees & Aspiring Entrepreneurs, and AI & Technology Resources.
- AI & Technology Support Section: "Better Connections. Smarter Resources. Greater Possibilities." with 3 editorial cards (AI & Productivity Tools, Company Offers & Resources, Startup & Ecosystem Support) and "Register Your Interest" CTA.
- Partnerships & Institutional Collaboration: Warm off-white section covering IITs/NITs/universities, E-Cells, AI tech providers, incubators/accelerators, and founder communities, with "Explore a Partnership" CTA.
- Early Interest Form: Clean form capturing name, role (Founder, Mentor, Student/Mentee, Other), email, and areas of interest with privacy reassurance.
- Partnership Inquiry Form: Form capturing organization name, category, contact person, email, and proposed collaboration.
- Final CTA & Launch Date: Closing section with October 20, 2026 launch highlight.
- Footer: Logo, tagline "People Move Potential Forward.", ecosystem overview, section links, official emails (community@veripeers.com, hello@veripeers.com, partnerships@veripeers.com, privacy@veripeers.com), privacy link, and copyright to Xplorevo Private Limited.
